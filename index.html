How it works

From messy to meaningful

Real shopping apps read millions of reviews. Before a computer can understand them, the data has to be cleaned. Here is the exact flow you will follow.

📥
step one

Collect raw data

Save reviews exactly as shoppers typed them.

“OMG these are SO COMFY!!! Best shoes ever! 🔥”
🧹
step two

Clean the data

Lowercase, and remove punctuation, symbols and emojis.

omg these are so comfy best shoes ever
🏷️
step three

Label the data

Mark each review 1 for positive or -1 for negative.

comfy shoes → 1 · sole broke → -1
⚖️
step four

Word weights

Give emotional words a score from -3 to +3.

comfy +2 · best +1 · broke -2 · disappointed -1
🧮
step five

Final score

Add up the weights of every known word.

comfy (+2) + best (+1) = +3 Positive
The intensity scale

How strong is a word?
